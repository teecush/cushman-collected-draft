# Cambridge clippings — 20 September 2026

Six previously untranscribed clippings from the family’s Trip 4 Cambridge PDFs are now part of the public export: five signed Robert Cushman pieces and one unsigned opera preview. They were already absent before the September redesign. The public archive grows from 3,206 to 3,212 entries.

Three dates come from handwritten annotations. Three clippings have no visible publication date and remain undated. The opera preview is explicitly marked “Unsigned preview; attribution to Robert Cushman is unconfirmed” in result cards, its reader and its static page; citations do not assign it to Robert. It is excluded from Article Spotlight. The alternate design uses the same authorship correction without redesigning its interface.

Early Writing retains its strict 1963–1966 dated selection, now five articles, followed by a separate “Undated Cambridge clippings” section linking the other three. All six are in the public catalog, text search, relevant entity indexes, static article pages and sitemap. Both New Cambridge and Broadsheet now contain four entries each. No dates or years have been inferred from performance dates.

Transcriptions were checked visually against the supplied clippings, joining column flow and line-break hyphenation. The two-page Verona review is one continuous article; the duplicate New Cambridge scan adds no extra records. One partially obscured word in Marching Song is supplied as [thematic], with an editorial note. No new raw PDFs or images are published.
